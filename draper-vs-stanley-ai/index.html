Draper vs Stanley

Two answers to the same problem: software you visit, or a hire that comes to you.

Updated 28 July 2026 · Written by the Draper team · Prices checked 28 July 2026

The short answer: Stanley and Draper solve the same problem from opposite ends. Stanley is software you visit: a web chat that learns your voice from your past LinkedIn posts and helps you write new ones for $149 a month. Draper is a hire that comes to you: it sits in your meetings, texts you the moment a call ends, interviews you by voice note in iMessage, WhatsApp or Telegram, and runs the schedule itself. Which one fits depends on where your best material lives.

Honesty first: Draper is our product. Stanley facts below were checked against Stan's own help centre and three independent reviews on 28 July 2026; where those sources disagree, we say so.

Where Stanley is the better choice

You can buy it today, for a start: Draper is in early access. If you already have a strong posting history, Stanley's analysis of what worked is genuinely useful, and its coaching emails teach as you go. If you prefer a tool you open deliberately rather than an assistant that messages you, the web-chat model may simply suit you better.

Where Draper is the better choice

If your best lines happen in meetings and voice notes rather than in a compose box, Stanley never hears them and Draper is built on them. If the discipline is your bottleneck, Draper owns the cadence and chases you, where Stanley waits for you to show up. And if you have little posting history, a past-post voice model has little to learn from, while Draper starts from how you actually talk.

The honest bottom line

Stanley made a real product in a category full of template machines, and at $149 it is priced like it. We built Draper because we think the deeper problem is not writing help, it is ownership: someone has to hear the good line, pull the story out of you, book the slot and refuse the filler. That is a hire, not a tab.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between Draper and Stanley?
Stanley is a $149/mo web chat that learns your voice from past LinkedIn posts and helps you draft new ones. Draper works over iMessage, WhatsApp or Telegram, builds posts from your meetings and voice notes, schedules to a cadence you set, and chases you rather than waiting.
Does Stanley schedule LinkedIn posts?
Independent reviewers consistently report Stanley has no scheduling and posting is manual, though Stanley's own launch materials mention publishing. Draper schedules to a cadence you set and skips slots when nothing is worth posting.
Is Draper more expensive than Stanley?
Stanley is $149 per month with a single plan and no trial. Draper is currently in early access via a waitlist, with a demo bookable with the founders.
