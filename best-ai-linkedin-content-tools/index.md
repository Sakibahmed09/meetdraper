# Best AI LinkedIn content tools for founders (2026)

Updated 28 July 2026. Prices checked against primary sources on the same date. Draper is our product and appears in this list; we disclose that up front.

**Short answer:** Taplio and Supergrow are the strongest self-drive web apps. Stanley ($149/mo) learns your voice from past posts via chat. Draper takes the whole job off you over iMessage/WhatsApp/Telegram, working from your meetings and voice notes.

| Tool | Price/mo | Interface | Voice learned from | Trial |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draper (ours) | $100 (early access) | iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram | Meetings + voice notes | Waitlist |
| Stanley | $149 | Web chat | Past LinkedIn posts | No |
| Taplio | $39 to $199 | Web app + extension | Viral-post database | 7 days |
| Supergrow | $19 to $139 | Web app | Content DNA + voice interview | 7 days |
| Kleo | $99 | Web app | Templates + inputs | No |
| ContentIn | ~$31+ | Web app + Telegram bot | Profile + past posts | 7 days |
| EasyGen | ~$60 (reported) | Web app | Creator formulas | 3 free posts |
| MagicPost | from $21 (promo) | Web app | Your inputs | Free trial |

Who should pick which: do-it-yourself → Supergrow or Taplio. Voice-from-past-posts via chat → Stanley. Everything off your plate, over text, from your meetings → Draper. A human → budget $1,500 to $3,500/mo.
