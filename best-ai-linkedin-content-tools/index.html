The tools, one by one

Draperour product$100/mo, early access

Draper is a content hire rather than a writing tool. It sits in on your meetings, texts you the second a call ends, interviews you by voice note in the chat you already use, then drafts, schedules and chases so posting stops living in your head. Built by a live ghostwriting agency, not a software team: the same people run LinkedIn by hand for 50+ founders (350M+ views, £20M attributed), and what that floor learns each week goes into the product. Early access is $100 a month, opened from the waitlist in small weekly cohorts.

Stanley$149/mo, no trial

Stanley, from the team behind Stan, calls itself an AI head of content. It connects to your LinkedIn, studies your past posts to learn your voice, and works through a web chat with an interview mode typed rather than spoken. Reviewers consistently note there is no scheduling, no extension and no team features, and it is the most expensive software option here.

Taplio$39 to $199/mo

The most complete traditional suite: scheduling, analytics, a viral-post database, a Chrome extension and lead-CRM tools. AI writing starts on the $69 Growth plan. The trade-offs are output that users on Reddit and Product Hunt describe as generic, and a 2.1 Trustpilot score driven by cancellation and support complaints. Best for operators who want an all-in-one dashboard.

Supergrow$19 to $139/mo

The best value web app in the category. Its Postcast feature runs a spoken AI interview, ten to fifteen minutes of talking becomes five to seven drafts, and Content DNA keeps a persistent voice profile. Analytics are thin and the design tools are young, but at $19 it is the sensible starting point for a hands-on founder.

Kleo$99/mo, no trial

Kleo began as a much-loved free Chrome extension, received a cease-and-desist from LinkedIn in 2025, and relaunched as a paid content platform with templates, graphics and weekly group coaching. The coaching and community are the real differentiators; the price is high for what is otherwise a template-led writing tool.

ContentInfrom about $31/mo

A solid AI ghostwriter web app that trains on your profile and improves as you edit. Notable for a Telegram companion bot that lets you write and schedule from your messages, the closest any tool here comes to Draper's home turf, though it remains a companion to the web app rather than the product itself.

EasyGenabout $60/mo as reported

Ruben Hassid's generator is trained on top-creator data and scans trends across Reddit, X and search. It produces posts fast and has voice dictation for capturing ideas. Critics find the output formulaic, purchases are final, and pricing is only visible after signup, which is why our figure is second-hand.

MagicPostfrom $21/mo on current promo

A newer analytics-plus-generation tool with carousels, an idea generator and an API. The independent review footprint is still small, so there is less outside evidence either way. Worth a look at the promotional price if analytics are your priority.

A human ghostwriter$800 to $6,000+/mo

Still the benchmark for judgment and accountability. Expect an onboarding interview, recurring calls to extract stories, and eight to twelve posts a month. The costs are the fee itself, dependency on one writer's availability, and voice drift when writers change. Our comparison of Draper against hiring a ghostwriter goes deeper.

Which should you pick?

  • You want to do it yourself with good software: Supergrow at $19, or Taplio if you also want CRM and lead tools.
  • You want your past posts mined for voice through chat: Stanley, if the $149 with no trial suits you.
  • You want it off your plate entirely, without hiring a person: that is Draper's territory: meetings in, posts out, everything over text.
  • You want a human: budget $1,500 to $3,500 a month for a mid-tier ghostwriter.

Questions people ask

What is the best AI LinkedIn content tool in 2026?
It depends on how much of the work you want to keep. Supergrow ($19/mo) and Taplio ($39+/mo) are the strongest self-serve web apps. Stanley ($149/mo) learns your voice from past posts through chat. Draper, our product, is the option built to take the whole job off you: it works from your meetings and voice notes over iMessage, WhatsApp or Telegram.
Which LinkedIn AI tools work over WhatsApp or iMessage?
Draper runs primarily over iMessage, WhatsApp and Telegram, with no separate app to open. Among the other tools compared here, ContentIn offers a Telegram companion bot alongside its web app. The rest are web apps or Chrome extensions.
Do any AI LinkedIn tools use your meetings as source material?
Of the tools in this comparison, only Draper ingests your meetings and turns what you actually said into posts. Supergrow's Postcast offers a spoken AI interview inside its web app, and Stanley offers a typed interview mode, but neither works from your real calls.

As of July 2026: Supergrow from $19/mo, ContentIn from about $31/mo, Taplio from $39/mo ($69 with AI writing), EasyGen about $60/mo as reported, Kleo $99/mo, Stanley $149/mo. Human ghostwriters typically run $800 to $6,000+ per month. Draper is $100 a month in early access, a third less than Stanley.
