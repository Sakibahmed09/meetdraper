# Draper vs hiring a LinkedIn ghostwriter (2026)

Updated 28 July 2026. Draper is our product; before that we ran the agency this comparison describes.

**Short answer:** a good ghostwriter costs $800 to $6,000+/mo, interviews you on calls, delivers 8 to 12 posts. Draper keeps the interviews, the ear and the quality bar, removes the calls, the wait and most of the cost.

| | Ghostwriter | Draper |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | $800 to $6,000+/mo | $100/mo, early access |
| Extraction | Scheduled calls | Your meetings + voice notes in texts |
| Turnaround | Days | When your call ends |
| Voice | Drifts with writer changes | Your own words |
| Quality bar | Writer-dependent | Skips unearned slots |

The human still wins on: senior judgment, industry relationships, taste over dinner. Draper wins on: speed, consistency, extraction where you already are, and saying no to filler.
