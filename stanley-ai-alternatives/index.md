# Stanley AI alternatives (2026)

Updated 28 July 2026. Draper is our product and leads this list; facts checked against primary sources.

Stanley: $149/mo, single plan, no trial, web-chat interface, learns your voice from past LinkedIn posts, typed interview mode. Common reasons to switch: price, no scheduling (per reviewers), no extension or team features, dependence on posting history.

Alternatives:
- **Draper (ours)**: early access. Works over iMessage/WhatsApp/Telegram; builds posts from your meetings and voice notes; drafts, schedules, chases.
- **Supergrow**: $19 to $139/mo. Spoken AI interview (Postcast), Content DNA, scheduling. Value pick.
- **Taplio**: $39 to $199/mo. Full suite + CRM. Note 2.1 Trustpilot (billing/support complaints).
- **ContentIn**: ~$31+/mo. Trains on your profile, Telegram companion bot.
- **Kleo**: $99/mo. Templates + weekly group coaching.
- **Human ghostwriter**: $800 to $6,000+/mo.
