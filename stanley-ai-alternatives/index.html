Stanley alternatives, compared honestly

Six places people go after Stanley, from a $19 web app to an assistant that lives in your texts.

Updated 28 July 2026 · Written by the Draper team · Prices checked 28 July 2026

The short answer: Stanley charges $149 a month, with one plan, no trial and a chat-only interface that learns your voice from your past LinkedIn posts. The alternatives below cover every direction people leave for: cheaper self-serve software, a suite with scheduling built in, a spoken interview, and, in Draper's case, an assistant that works from your meetings over the messaging apps you already use.

Honesty first: Draper is our product and it leads this list. Every fact about the other tools was checked against primary sources on 28 July 2026.

What Stanley does well, and where people look elsewhere

Stanley, built by the team behind Stan, analyses your posting history, identifies what performed, and drafts new posts in that voice through a conversational interface. Its typed Interview Me mode pulls stories out of you with questions. That analysis-first approach is genuinely good, and the coaching emails are a nice touch.

The reasons people search for alternatives are consistent across reviews: the $149 price with no trial, the absence of scheduling (reviewers report all posting is manual, though Stanley's own launch materials mention publishing, so we hedge here), no Chrome extension, no team features, and a voice model built on your past posts, which is a problem if you have not posted much worth learning from.

Supergrow$19 to $139/mo

The value pick, and the closest feature match to Stanley's interviewing instinct: Postcast runs a spoken AI interview where ten to fifteen minutes of talking becomes five to seven drafts. Add persistent Content DNA voice profiles, scheduling and a carousel maker, at roughly an eighth of Stanley's price with a seven-day trial.

Taplio$39 to $199/mo

The all-in-one operator suite: scheduling, analytics, viral-post research, Chrome extension, lead CRM. AI writing needs the $69 plan. Be aware of the 2.1 Trustpilot score, largely about billing and support rather than the product's capability.

ContentInfrom about $31/mo

Trains on your profile and improves as you edit, with unlimited scheduling and a Telegram companion bot for writing from your messages. A sensible mid-point between Stanley's voice focus and Taplio's tooling.

Kleo$99/mo

Templates, graphics and weekly group coaching. Pick it for the coaching community more than the AI. No trial, like Stanley.

A human ghostwriter$800 to $6,000+/mo

The original alternative. Real judgment and accountability, at five to forty times Stanley's price, with turnaround measured in days and quality that varies wildly by writer.

How to choose

  • Loved Stanley's interview idea but want it spoken and cheaper: Supergrow.
  • Need scheduling, analytics and lead tools in one place: Taplio.
  • Want the interview to happen in your texts, sourced from your real meetings: Draper.
  • Have little posting history for an AI to learn from: avoid past-post models entirely; Draper and a human ghostwriter both build voice from conversation instead.
