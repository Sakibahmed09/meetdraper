Privacy

Last updated 2 July 2026

The short version

This is an early-access landing page. It does not set cookies, run analytics, or track you across the web.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, the email address you enter is stored so we can contact you about early access. Nothing else is collected. If you never touch the form, we hold nothing about you.

What we do with it

One thing: email you when your seat opens. We do not sell, share, or enrich your email, and we do not add you to any other list.

Third parties

Fonts are served by Google Fonts, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Both may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of serving content. Their own privacy policies apply to that.

Removal

Want your email gone? Write to sakib@withsignal.io and it will be deleted.
